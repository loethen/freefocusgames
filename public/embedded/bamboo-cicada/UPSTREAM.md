# Upstream attribution

This embedded game is adapted from
[imsai-sh/zhuzhiliao](https://github.com/imsai-sh/zhuzhiliao) at commit
`567508d35e1a91bb376e01e620c21587f3a3146a`.

The original fixed-step rope physics, sampled audio and synthesized fallback,
Canvas renderer, Three.js model, automatic swing mode, pointer controls, and
device-motion controls are retained.

FreeFocusGames changes are limited to internationalized interface copy,
same-site embedding, removal of the upstream telemetry/global visitor and
"wah" counters, removal of the WebSocket/Worker connection, and removal of
standalone PWA installation UI.

The upstream repository did not include a detected license at the time of this
adaptation. Publication should be preceded by confirmation from the original
author that the code, recording, and 3D assets may be redistributed.
