**On our 5×5 Schulte Table, the typical player's best time is 26.4 seconds. Finishing in under 20 seconds puts you ahead of about 79% of players, and under 15 seconds puts you in the top 5%.**

These numbers come from 10,560 players of our [Schulte Table](/games/schulte-table) between March and October 2026. Each player counts once, with their best time.

## Schulte Table time percentiles (5×5)

The time is how long it takes to tap 1 to 25 in order. Each wrong tap adds 2 seconds.

| Percentile | Best time | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top 1% | 11.6 s | Exceptional |
| Top 5% | 15.1 s | Very fast |
| Top 10% | 17.1 s | Fast |
| Top 25% | 20.8 s | Above average |
| Median | 26.4 s | Half of players are faster, half slower |
| Bottom 25% | 35.5 s | Slower than average |
| Bottom 10% | 47.3 s | Slow, often a first try |

## What changes your time

- **Mistakes cost a lot.** Each wrong tap adds 2 seconds, so a single slip can push a 19-second run past 21.
- **Search strategy.** A common approach is to keep your eyes near the center of the grid and use peripheral vision instead of scanning row by row.
- **Practice on the same grid.** Times usually drop quickly over the first few attempts as you learn the task, then level off.
- **Device.** A larger screen and a mouse make it easier to see and hit the right cell than a small phone.

## How we calculated these numbers

- **Data:** best 5×5 times from 10,628 players on FreeFocusGames, March 8 to October 25, 2026, including the 2-second penalty for each mistake.
- **Exclusions:** times under 8 seconds (very likely not genuine) and over 120 seconds (usually an interrupted game). That left 10,560 players.
- **Limits:** players chose to play, use different devices, and each counts with their best time, so this is a practical benchmark for this grid, not a scientific population average.

## FAQ

**What is a good time for a 5×5 Schulte Table?**
Under 20 seconds is good: it beats about 79% of players on our grid. Under 15 seconds is excellent.

**What is the average Schulte Table time?**
The median best time on our 5×5 grid is 26.4 seconds and the mean is 30.2 seconds.

**Do mistakes count?**
Yes. Each wrong tap adds 2 seconds to your time, so accuracy matters as much as speed.
